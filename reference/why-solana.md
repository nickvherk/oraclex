# Why Solana

Prediction intelligence operates at machine speed, not human speed.

The next generation of prediction systems depends on real-time signals, autonomous agents, cross-market intelligence, low-latency execution, and coordinated market resolution. These systems are not passive dashboards. They are infrastructure surfaces for routing information, pricing uncertainty, and triggering downstream action across markets.

OracleX is designed for that environment.

Prediction market coordination requires infrastructure that can support frequent updates, fast settlement paths, and high-volume interaction between agents, markets, and data networks. Latency compounds quickly when signals move across venues, models, wallets, and execution layers.

Solana provides the operating characteristics required for this class of system:

- sub-second execution
- high throughput
- scalable coordination
- low-latency infrastructure

OracleX uses Solana because prediction intelligence benefits from a network where coordination can happen at the speed of the underlying information flow.

Prediction markets are becoming the intelligence layer of the internet.

OracleX is building the intelligence layer for prediction markets.

Both belong on Solana.
